# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Product Purpose
SPS, Service Petroleum and Supply C.A., presents petroleum and industrial services in Venezuela. The user requests a brand-new independent complete website using the approved full-page compositions, making its experience and years in the field visible.
## Users
Inferred from existing content: industrial and petroleum operations teams evaluating a service provider and requesting technical assistance.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve the six main routes: Inicio, Nosotros, Servicios, Proyectos, Artículos, Contacto. The replacement implementation covers 22 service details, six project details and six article details, for 40 content routes. Preserve all service offerings, project evidence, contact numbers, address, company history and brand logo. Root index.html boots src/experience/app.js: vanilla JavaScript and CSS served/built by Vite. The active modules are src/experience/app.js, gallery.js, style.css, gallery.css, network.js, content.js, catalogue.js and icons.js. Older src/site and src/web implementations remain archival and are not the root rendering stack. gallery.js now provides page-specific compositions for five main pages and all three detail types; the contact page remains in app.js, with gallery.css loaded after style.css. The contact form downloads a local text summary; it has no delivery backend and must not claim submissions are received.
## Evidence on Hand
src/experience/content.js supplies service, project and article records; src/experience/catalogue.js preserves the service groups. Supplied project photography is copied into public/assets/experience/field from public/assets/sps-field. Foundation in 1990 is a repository claim, not an independently verified fact. The original logo is public/assets/sps-oil-logo.png; the active copy is public/assets/experience/logo.png. Contact numbers and the Zulia address are rendered by src/experience/app.js. Generated assets under public/assets/experience are conceptual illustration and decorative equipment, not additional evidence of completed work.
## Brand Commitments
SPS name and logo. Spanish language. User wants a professional presentation emphasizing experience and longevity. The 62 approved full-page compositions in design-proposals/secciones-2026-09-29 are the current visual authority, superseding the earlier nine-page reference set. The independent implementation and its connected photographic pipe network remain under visual review; documentation does not certify fidelity.
## Product Principles
Show actual field evidence. Make service scope clear. Make contact honest and actionable. Preserve content without inventing certifications or achievements.
