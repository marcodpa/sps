# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Product Purpose
SPS, Service Petroleum and Supply C.A., presents petroleum and industrial services in Venezuela. The user requests a professional redesign of the entire site that makes its experience and years in the field visible.
## Users
Inferred from existing content: industrial and petroleum operations teams evaluating a service provider and requesting technical assistance.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve the five routes: Home, Nosotros, Servicios, Proyectos, Contacto. Preserve all service offerings, project evidence, contact numbers, address, company history and brand logo. Existing React/Vite application. The current contact form has no delivery backend; do not claim submissions are received.
## Evidence on Hand
src/data/spsContent.js contains three service groups and six project cases. public/assets/sps-field contains supplied project photography. Existing Nosotros page states foundation in 1990 and milestones in 2000, 2010, 2020. These are repository claims, not independently verified facts. Existing logos in public/assets/client-logos. Contact numbers and Zulia address in Contacto.jsx.
## Brand Commitments
SPS name and logo. Spanish language. User wants a professional presentation emphasizing experience and longevity.
## Product Principles
Show actual field evidence. Make service scope clear. Make contact honest and actionable. Preserve content without inventing certifications or achievements.
