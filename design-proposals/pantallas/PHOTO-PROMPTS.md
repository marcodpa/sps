## Panorama
Use case: lighting-weather / photographic retouch. Edit the supplied ACTUAL company field photograph with conservative professional exposure and color correction only, for a corporate website. Preserve the same photograph, camera viewpoint, composition, ALL equipment, exact piping, tanks, boiler, metal shed, yellow handrails, ground, labels, logos, people and surroundings exactly. No added or removed objects, no invented equipment, no new text, no replacements or repainting. Recover overly bright highlights and shadows, neutralize cyan/yellow cast, natural daylight, slightly clearer microcontrast, careful mild sharpening without halos, natural realistic sky, no dramatic cinematic grading, no HDR look, do not make equipment newer. Keep true colors and proportions. Original aspect ratio. Fully opaque photograph, edge to edge, no transparency. The purpose is faithful photographic improvement, not creative regeneration.
